Control air system for a hydrodynamic retarder

Inventors: Mirco Wassermann; Christoph Sachs; Alexander Dederer

Publication number: EP4522467A1

Authority: European Patent Office

Publication date: 2025-03-19

Abstract

This patent publication describes a control air system for a hydrodynamic retarder. The system comprises pneumatic channels and valve arrangements for controlling pressure, venting, and switching behavior in connection with retarder operation.
