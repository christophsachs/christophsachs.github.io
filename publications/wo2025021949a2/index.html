Quick-filling valve device

Inventors: Christoph Sachs; Alexander Dederer

Publication number: WO2025021949A2

Authority: WIPO / PCT

Application number: PCT/EP2024/071193

Publication date: 2025-01-30

Priority date: 2023-07-25

Assignee: Norgren GmbH

Abstract

This patent publication describes a quick-filling valve device for supplying pressurized fluid to a working means. The supply of pressurized fluid is realized by a quick-filling valve which is pilot controlled by a control valve. The opening and closing of the quick-filling valve are caused by a membrane movable between a first position and a second position. The valve structure provides a large flow cross-section for pressurized fluid supplied from a pressure source to the working means.

Technical field

Pneumatic and fluid-pressure actuator systems, quick-filling valves, pilot-controlled valves, membrane valves, pressure supply systems, and fail-safe actuator control.
