Control air system and method for controlling a control air system

Inventors: Christian Schneider; Christoph Sachs

Publication number: EP4077072A1

Authority: European Patent Office

Publication date: 2022-10-26

Priority date: 2019-12-18

Abstract

This publication describes a control air system for providing pressurised air with an adjustable control air pressure in an air chamber, preferably for controlling the braking torque of a hydrodynamic retarder. The system includes a control connection, a pressurised air supply line, electrically actuatable inlet valves, a venting line, an electrically actuatable venting valve, sensor elements for pressure detection, and a control device for controlling the control air pressure.
